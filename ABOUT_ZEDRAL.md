# About Zedral

## 🚀 The Vision: Operational Efficiency Intelligence
Zedral is an advanced operational intelligence platform designed specifically for manufacturing and industrial environments. We transform raw video data from existing shop floor infrastructure into actionable insights, moving beyond simple reporting to true operational intelligence.

In modern industry, efficiency isn't just a metric; it's a competitive advantage. Zedral provides the visibility needed to identify, measure, and eliminate inefficiencies in real-time.

---

## 🏗️ Core Problem: The Tech Gap on the Shop Floor
Most manufacturing floors suffer from "Operational Blindness":
- **Manual Tracking:** Relying on human observation for SOP compliance and bottleneck detection.
- **Lagging Indicators:** Knowing about production delays hours or days after they occur.
- **Hardware Friction:** Traditional IoT solutions often require expensive, intrusive new hardware installations.

---

## 🛠️ The Zedral Solution: AI + Existing Infrastructure
Zedral uses cutting-edge **Computer Vision and AI** to bridge this gap without requiring a single new camera.

### 1. Workstation Activity Tracking
- Monitor workstation uptime and downtime automatically.
- Analyze cycle times for every operator and machine.
- Identify micro-stoppages that aggregate into major productivity losses.

### 2. Bottleneck Detection & Resolution
- Real-time heatmaps of production flow.
- Automated alerts when a station lags behind the tactical time.
- Data-backed insights to rebalance lines dynamically.

### 3. SOP & Safety Compliance
- Automated monitoring of Standard Operating Procedures.
- Detection of safety gear (PPE) compliance.
- Real-time intervention alerts to prevent quality defects or accidents.

---
